<objective>
Make the candidate a candidate: something that compiles, resolves, and whose money-math and property suites are green against the post-merge dependency set.

Purpose: D-17 identifies the dominant hazard as the one `git merge` cannot see. The `origin/main` side moved Decimal from `~> 2.0` to `~> 3.0` and ex_money from `~> 5.24` to `~> 6.2` with explicit `ex_cldr`/`ex_cldr_numbers`, and Ecto from 3.13.6 to 3.14.2. Nearly five hundred commits of milestone work — all money math and all StreamData property tests — have never been compiled against that set. Both parents are green, the merge is textually clean, and the result has never been compiled: the classic merge-queue failure. D-19 makes fixing what Decimal 3 breaks in scope, because a candidate nobody can compile is not a candidate and deferring hands Phase 231 a fresh-checkout gate that fails for untriaged reasons.

Output: a committed `.tool-versions`, four re-resolved lockfiles, a compiling candidate, green money-math and property suites with captured exit codes, and focused regressions for both surviving hunks of the `config.ex` hazard.
</objective>

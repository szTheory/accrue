<objective>
Ship the HYG-01 classification as a fail-closed `collect`/`render`/`verify` triad matching the four
existing precedents, and mint the committed record before any cleanup touches the repository.

Purpose: D-45 through D-54, with D-47's structural no-deletion invariant and D-52's not-a-typo naming convention both binding. A prose classification rots the moment cleanup runs. Failing closed in only
one direction rots more subtly: a record that is merely complete becomes fiction as soon as an item is
removed, and a record that is merely sound hides an item nobody classified. The no-deletion decision is
encoded structurally rather than as an intention, so the verifier cannot express the forbidden action.

Output: three new scripts, a committed JSON record and its deterministic Markdown projection, and a
merge-blocking CI step.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the `collect`/`render`/`verify` triad shape itself, of which this phase adds a
fifth instance.

**Decision:** `add-alongside`.

**Rationale:** the four existing triads share a shape, not a runtime. Their overlap is small and mostly
mechanical (the exact-map join helper, the sanitization pattern, the phase-evidence-path resolver), and
each of those three is already extracted into a module or copied with an explicit provenance comment.
Promoting the whole triad to a shared framework now would couple five artifacts with different schemas
and different strict flags to one abstraction on the strength of five samples, in the same change that
already rewrites one of them. This plan therefore imports the shared helpers and copies the shape.

**What would force a later promote:** a sixth triad, or any single defect that has to be fixed in three
or more triads at once. Either is the signal that the duplication has started costing more than the
coupling would.
</assumption_delta_decision>

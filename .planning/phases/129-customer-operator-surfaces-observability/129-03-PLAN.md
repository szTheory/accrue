<objective>
Render a conditional recovery banner in the portal subscription LiveView so a customer with a failed payment is prompted to fix it, with a provider-aware deep-link into the existing add/update-payment-method flow.

Purpose: DUN-06 / SC#1. No new route, no new layout slot, no new component — the banner is a conditional `<section class="portal-card">` inserted into the existing render tree (D-09), gated on already-loaded subscription state (D-10), with a provider-aware CTA (D-11). Output: the banner markup + two private helpers (`recovery_prompt?/1`, `update_pm_path/2`), three new `AccruePortal.Copy` strings, and LiveView render tests proving show/hide and provider-correct CTA hrefs.
</objective>

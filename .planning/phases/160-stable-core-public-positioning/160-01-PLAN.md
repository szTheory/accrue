| T-160-02 | Repudiation | Package READMEs and proof mirrors | mitigate | Keep admin/portal/host/adoption docs as thin mirrors with explicit pointers back to `first_hour.md`, `jobs_to_be_done.md`, and `maturity-and-maintenance.md`, preventing support-boundary overstatement. |

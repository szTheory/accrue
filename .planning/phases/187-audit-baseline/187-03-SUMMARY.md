## Accomplishments

- Added `admin-baseline.spec.js`, which imports `baseline-manifest.js`, seeds deterministic E2E fixtures, visits manifest surfaces, captures light/dark screenshots, runs axe, and writes raw observations to `accrue_admin/test-results/admin-baseline/{project}/cells.json`.

- Added targeted breakpoint probes at 320, 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 for manifest surfaces with responsive/layout risk metadata without multiplying every state by every width.

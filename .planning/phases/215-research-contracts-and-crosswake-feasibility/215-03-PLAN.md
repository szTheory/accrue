|---|---|
| research prose → executable case corpus | Ambiguous prose becomes data consumed by later entitlement code and clients. |
| Elixir corpus → generated JSON/Markdown | Export drift or a second reducer could change semantics across consumers. |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-215-09 | Tampering | generated fixtures | high | mitigate | `mix accrue.entitlements.decision_cases --check` and mutation-sensitive tests fail closed on byte, ID, version, or disposition drift. |
| T-215-10 | Elevation of Privilege | case interpretation | high | mitigate | `decision_cases_test.exs` fails if renderer/exporter output diverges from case data or the corpus exposes a reducer/projector/public facade. |
| T-215-11 | Repudiation | support outcomes | medium | mitigate | Stable case and reason IDs plus version fields identify every expected result. |
| T-215-12 | Information Disclosure | case evidence/reasons | medium | mitigate | Schema admits bounded provenance and privacy-safe reason IDs, not raw provider payloads or PII. |

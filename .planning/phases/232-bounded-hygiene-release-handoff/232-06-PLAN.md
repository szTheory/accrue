<objective>
Split the parked Admin UI ratchet lane so its machinery stays gated while its subject is parked,
replace the unconditional fabricated summary with one that reads real numbers, and record the parked
state once, in the ledger that already enforces waiver completeness.

Purpose: D-21 through D-26. The job currently sets `continue-on-error` at job level, which bundles
three genuinely passing self-test steps with the two parked ones — so a real regression in the ratchet
machinery would be silently absorbed. Its status summary hardcodes eight passing rows and is currently
unreachable, which means it starts printing fabricated passes the moment the gate ahead of it goes
green: the dead-branch-that-lies pattern in its purest form.

Output: two jobs where there was one, a real summary step, an expiry trigger, a disposition-keyed
sweep exclusion, and one waived ship-window row.
</objective>

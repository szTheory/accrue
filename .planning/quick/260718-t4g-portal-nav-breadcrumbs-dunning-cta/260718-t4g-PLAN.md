# Quick Task 260718-t4g — Plan

## Goal

Two combined scopes, each with its own atomic commit:

**PART A (library, `accrue_portal`):** the customer portal has a shallow 2-level page hierarchy but no way
back — no back button, breadcrumb, or home link on `/billing/payment-methods`, `/billing/subscriptions`,
`/billing/subscriptions/:id`, or `/billing/invoices`, and the topbar logo isn't a link. Ship a really-good
default: location-aware breadcrumbs on the 5 sub-pages + a clickable brand → home, themeable via the existing
`--accrue-*` tokens.

**PART B (host/demo-only, `examples/accrue_host`):** the `/app/billing` dunning banner is styled but has no
actionable link ("Action required — payment failed" with nowhere to go). Add a CTA to the in-portal
payment-methods page.

This plan mirrors the authoritative design at `~/.claude/plans/i-m-in-users-jon-projects-accrue-current-refactored-willow.md`
exactly. Do not expand scope, add a top-nav tab bar, convert `<a href>` full-loads to `live_redirect`, or touch
the theme picker.

       per item, an `<a :if={crumb.href} href={crumb.href} class="portal-breadcrumb-link">{crumb.label}</a>`

## Reassessment protocol

1. Record the trigger date, source/provenance, affected claim ID, owner, and proposed response in the relevant watchlist row or linked incident/runbook record.
2. Reassess against the precedence in [v1.59-AUTHORITY.md](v1.59-AUTHORITY.md); do not treat the trigger as policy acceptance.
3. If accepted policy changes, add a new effective-dated ledger row that identifies the superseded claim/location and downstream tests. Preserve the previous row and source text.

## Change and review rule

A provider notice, dependency change, or [watchlist](v1.59-WATCHLIST.md) trigger opens a **dated reassessment** owned by the listed phase/runbook. It may update evidence and the amendment ledger, but it cannot silently alter entitlement behavior. Routine findings use the ledger; standalone ADRs/RFCs are reserved for broad, stable public-contract decisions.

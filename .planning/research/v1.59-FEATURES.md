# v1.59 Feature Landscape: Account-Scoped Multi-Rail & Offline Entitlements

**Project:** Accrue
**Domain:** Phoenix billing library support for a B2C account spanning Stripe web billing, Apple IAP, and offline-first study
**Researched:** 2026-07-31
**Overall confidence:** MEDIUM — the policy is backed by current Apple, Stripe, and IETF primary sources plus the accepted project architecture; Crosswake-specific implementation remains a host concern to validate in phase work.

## Product Position

The product is not a generic “subscriptions across every storefront” layer. It is an account-entitlement projection: verified Stripe and Apple evidence independently grants a logical plan to one Accrue account; the resulting access snapshot is rail-neutral, but billing management remains candidly rail-specific. This serves B2C Alpha as an anonymized language-learning reference host without encoding its identity, user data, or business rules in Accrue.

The learner promise is intentionally narrow and testable: a verified, entitled learner may continue using already-downloaded study content and local progress after a 30-day lease revalidation target becomes stale, while new premium downloads or other value-expanding actions wait for reconnection. There is no independent 72-hour cutoff. That avoids connectivity-driven lockout without representing stale offline proof as new billing truth.

## Differentiators

| Feature | Value proposition | Complexity | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Account projection separated from rail lifecycle | Simple host access checks without a dishonest lowest-common-denominator billing API. | High | The key OSS-maintainer choice: access becomes portable, lifecycle semantics remain visible. |
| Signed device-bound offline lease | Supports serious offline study while limiting casual token copying and enabling explicit reconnect state. | High | Device binding is a containment measure, not DRM or compromise detection. |
| Revision/tombstone reconciliation | A reconnect can atomically replace old access with a new lease or signed denial after refund/revocation. | High | Prevents a stale positive token from winning a local selection race. |
| Duplicate policy without forced migration | Protects customers from a mistaken second purchase while avoiding accidental cancellation, pro-rating, and finance liability. | Medium | A support-friendly boundary for B2C Alpha’s first multi-rail release. |
| Source-aware diagnostics and public contract | Lets adopters and operators answer “why access changed?” while retaining the v1.58 rule that Stripe-native advisory cache is never a gate. | Medium | Include runbooks, support matrix, examples, fixtures, telemetry, and release notes together. |
| Reference-host proof | Demonstrates the Phoenix + Crosswake handoff rather than implying that a backend-only library solves secure mobile storage/UI. | Medium | Must remain anonymized and free of actual adopter PII/identity. |

## Anti-Features (Binding v1.59 Non-Goals)

| Anti-feature | Why avoid | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play rail | Android is not scheduled and has different lifecycle/backend semantics. | Keep SEED-007 deferred; define rail seams without shipping Android policy. |
| Fake universal cancel/swap/dunning API | Apple management is externally controlled; pretending parity causes dangerous operations and misleading docs. | Expose per-rail capabilities plus externally-managed guidance. |
| Automatic ownership transfer, migration, or cross-rail proration | Creates financial, refund, tax, and support obligations without verified ownership policy. | Warn/block duplicate purchase; route exceptions to a documented support runbook. |
| Email/device/product-only Apple matching | These are ambiguous and spoofable links. | Bind verified evidence to authenticated `appAccountToken` only; quarantine unmatched evidence. |
| Making Stripe `EntitlementSummary` a universal gate | v1.58 expressly made it observational-only; changing this breaks correctness and isolation guarantees. | Use the new canonical account snapshot for v1.59 access. |
| Arbitrary TTL/risk knobs | A broad configuration matrix obscures the risk contract and multiplies QA/support states. | Ship the locked 30-day revalidation target and one stale-offline continuity policy; defer a general risk matrix. |
| Family Sharing and offer authoring | Apple ownership and offer semantics require deliberate policy, not an accidental side effect of account union. | Represent provenance where possible; defer behavior. |
| PII, receipts, or raw provider payloads in lease/telemetry | Increases privacy breach surface and violates minimum-data operational design. | Store bounded evidence; use opaque IDs and redacted diagnostics. |

## Explicit Policy Decisions

| Decision | Approved v1.59 policy | Stakeholder consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Reference host | Anonymized B2C Alpha language-learning host only. | OSS docs prove integration without exposing adopter identity or learner data. |
| Rails | Stripe + Apple concurrently; Google Play deferred. | Backend/adopter gets scope certainty; mobile team targets iOS only. |
| Entitlement aggregation | Union effective grants by account; duplicate equivalent plans do not stack; features set-union and quantities max. | Learner keeps access if either valid source remains; finance/support can still see source provenance. |
| Apple identity | Accrue account UUID is supplied and verified as `appAccountToken`; no heuristic links. | Security/privacy get deterministic, auditable linkage. |
| Duplicate purchase | Warn/block before a second equivalent subscription; no automatic cancellation, migration, refund, transfer, or proration. | Product avoids surprise charge; support has a manual exception path. |
| Lifecycle management | Stripe is controllable; Apple is observed and externally managed. | UI/API must declare capability rather than present broken controls. |
| Offline freshness | Rolling 30 days after successful reconciliation; scheduled ends can shorten it. | Learners can travel/offline-study; product accepts bounded stale-access exposure. |
| Offline stale behavior | Continue downloaded study and local progress; wait for reconnect for new premium downloads/value expansion. | No connectivity lockout of core learning, no new paid-value leakage. |
| Stale-offline policy | Keep downloaded study and progress available, fail closed for new premium downloads/value expansion, and show reconnect-required guidance. | Avoid connectivity-driven lockout and destructive data hiding while keeping stale proof from expanding paid value. |
| Provider grace | Project Apple grace/retry bounds; do not stack offline grace after provider grace. | Finance prevents double grace; QA has one temporal policy to assert. |
| Privacy | No PII/adopter identity/receipt bodies in planning, fixtures, leases, telemetry, or diagnostics. | Legal/security surface is constrained by design. |

## Stakeholder Acceptance Criteria

| Lens | Must be true at milestone acceptance |
|---|---|
| OSS maintainer | Public APIs/config are additive; old Stripe/default-rail behavior, support matrix, examples, verifier, and release notes agree; no breaking map-processor conversion. |
| Adopter backend engineer | Can register both rails, map rail-qualified products to logical plans, request one account snapshot, reconcile verified Apple evidence idempotently, and recover missed provider events. |
| Crosswake/mobile engineer | Receives a documented compact ES256 lease contract, verification fixtures and JWKS/key identifiers; stores key material/lease securely; never treats a local lease as server billing evidence; renders fresh, stale, and reconnect-required modes. |
| Product owner | User stories demonstrate web purchase → iOS access, iOS purchase → web access, accidental second-purchase warning/block, and offline progress continuation with explicit value-expansion gating. |
| Learner | Uses a single account across web/iOS, knows where to manage each purchase, can restore legitimately owned Apple access, and never loses local progress merely because connectivity is absent. |
| Billing/support operator | Can identify governing rail, provider and normalized state, observed time, source IDs/redacted correlation, account revision, lease horizon, and the next repair/support action. |
| Security/abuse | Rejects unsigned/wrong-algorithm/wrong-issuer/wrong-audience/wrong-device/expired/replayed-or-rolled-back lease cases; rejects unverified Apple claims; preserves rail+environment namespace; has signed deny-tombstone handling. |
| Privacy/legal | Fixture/docs/telemetry scans establish no adopter identity, learner PII, receipts, or raw notification bodies in new surfaces; management language reflects Apple/Stripe terms and responsibilities. |
| Finance | No cross-rail cancel/refund/proration/migration is invoked; one source revocation retracts only that grant; grace/retry/cancellation boundaries are represented without double grace. |
| SRE | Observes verification, projection, repair, issuance, refresh, and denial outcomes with redacted telemetry; has retry/backoff/dead-letter/quarantine and alert/runbook coverage for Apple/Stripe delivery failure. |
| QA/release | Covers concurrency and idempotency, out-of-order/duplicate events, sandbox-vs-production isolation, refunds/revocations/grace/retry, key rotation, clock rollback, device revocation, offline state matrix, compatibility, and reference-host E2E proof. |
| OSS docs | Documents exact support boundary, capability matrix, integration sequence, security/privacy constraints, support playbooks, known non-goals, and Stripe advisory-cache isolation. |

## Feature Dependencies

```text
Account identity + rail-qualified mapping
  → verified Stripe/Apple observers and idempotent evidence reduction
  → canonical account grant projection + revision
  → duplicate-purchase eligibility + rail-aware management capability
  → lease issuance/reconnect/tombstones
  → Crosswake offline UX + operator diagnostics + B2C Alpha proof
```

## MVP Recommendation

Prioritize:

1. Account identity, rail registration, qualified mapping, verified Apple ingest/repair, and canonical union snapshot.
2. Provider-honest capabilities plus duplicate-purchase warning/block and support diagnostics.
3. ES256 device lease/reconnect policy with Crosswake reference-host proof and the offline state matrix.

Defer Google Play, Family Sharing behavior, offer authoring, generic migration/proration, transfer/merge tooling, hardware attestation, advanced fraud controls, and policy knobs. Each needs a concrete adopter and separate lifecycle/financial/privacy decision.

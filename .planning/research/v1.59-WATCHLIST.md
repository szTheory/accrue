## Review cadence

- At dependency update, provider API-version change, and release candidate: execute the relevant row’s gates and record the dated reassessment.
- Weekly while Apple repair is in rollout: inspect verification/quarantine/retry/backlog age and stale-lease/deny rates.
- Quarterly and after a security incident: run a key-rotation tabletop, dependency-advisory review, and Crosswake contract retest.

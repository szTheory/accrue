### The GATE-01 / GATE-02 reading, stated plainly

GATE-01 and GATE-02 score `satisfied` because REQUIREMENTS.md asks for **honest per-lane proof**, not a green result — "with no aggregate green/passing boolean and no fabricated `proved` state." The phase delivered exactly that: 19 declared lanes, 11 `proved`, 1 real `failed`, 1 `advisory`, 6 `non_run`/`skipped` each with a named reason, plus a real (not simulated) `workflow_dispatch` run `35100620086` recorded with its actual `conclusion: failure`.

That wording is itself a mid-milestone correction. Quick-task `260916-gda` (commit `06a7c863`) reworded both requirements away from "are green" *precisely because* Phase 231's locked decision D-29 forbids an aggregate green boolean. So the requirement as written is met, and met in the spirit it was rewritten to express.

`231-VERIFICATION.md` scores these "⚠️ Complete-as-process, not complete-as-outcome" and says so in its own Central Judgment section rather than laundering it. That is the correct call, and this audit adopts it.
